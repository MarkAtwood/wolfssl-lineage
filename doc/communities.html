Porting

We are also actively recruiting community members who are interested in porting OpenSSL based projects to yaSSL using our portability layer. We think through the process of completing additional OpenSSL to yaSSL ports that we can incrementally improve the compatibility layer for all.

Should you need to contact us regarding any community related issues, email us at community(at)yassl.com.
